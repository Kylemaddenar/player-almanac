# Bundled typography

Manrope by the Manrope Project Authors and Fraunces by the Fraunces Project Authors are bundled under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their supplied license files are preserved alongside the fonts; Manrope's FONTLOG is included.

- Manrope source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope
- Fraunces source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/fraunces

Downloaded from the official Google Fonts repository on September 11, 2026. The font binaries are unmodified. The app loads these local files and precaches them for offline use.
