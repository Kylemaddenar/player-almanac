# Player Almanac - First-use walkthrough

**7.9.0-folio.2**

Designed for about five minutes, not a timed usability-study result. Choose one path; you are never required to create and import. Existing characters may be reviewed without changing them. A practice dice roll is optional and creates a real roll-history entry, not damage.

**Back / Next** navigate instructions. **Skip this step** moves on without performing its action. **Close walkthrough** keeps a resumable place. **Restart** resets only instructions. During a native dialog, finish or close that dialog before moving to another step; closing the walkthrough does not close the dialog. On very short viewports the companion becomes an inline panel so its actions remain reachable.

Creation and import advance only after the original operation succeeds. Export reports a download request, not proof that a backup reached disk. Check the actual saved file.

## Step content

### Your Library, your starting point

Choose how to begin below: create, import, or use a character already in this Library.

**Why it matters:** Characters stay in this browser/profile and app location. There is no cloud account; an exported file is your independent backup.

**Control or area:** **Library**

**Expected result:** You select one path; you will not be asked to do both creation and import.

### Make your own blank sheet

Choose **Create character** → **Start with a blank sheet**, fill the creation form, then choose **Create character** in that form.

**Why it matters:** This creates a real record only when you confirm. Enter the identity you know; statistics come next.

**Control or area:** **Create character** / **Start with a blank sheet** / creation form

**Expected result:** Your new character opens in **Identity** and the walkthrough moves on.

### Bring an existing character

Choose **Import**, select a JSON file or paste it, then **Preview import**; review it and confirm **Import**.

**Why it matters:** Keep **Add as new characters** to make a separate copy. Review warnings and required acknowledgements; do not replace your Library just to learn.

**Control or area:** **Import** / **Preview import**

**Expected result:** The import completes and you return to the Library with the imported card.

### Open the folio

Choose **Open folio** on the character you want to learn with.

**Why it matters:** The walkthrough follows that character without replacing any of its values.

**Control or area:** **Open folio** on a Library card

**Expected result:** **Play** opens for that character. Choose Next to review its setup.

### Give the folio its identity

In **Identity**, review **Character name**, **Species / ancestry** and **Class / subclass**; enter anything you know, then leave the field.

**Why it matters:** Ordinary fields commit when you leave them. Class text is descriptive; it does not build statistics for you.

**Control or area:** **Character name** in **Identity**

**Expected result:** The header reflects a committed name and the save status shows the result. Existing values may be left unchanged.

### Check your core abilities

In **Abilities & training**, review the six scores and selected saving-throw/skill proficiencies; enter only known values.

**Why it matters:** Scores and proficiency choices are yours; the app calculates ability modifiers and related check/save bonuses.

**Control or area:** **Abilities & training** editor

**Expected result:** You can identify your scores and their derived modifiers. You do not need to change an existing character to continue.

### Make HP ready for play

In **Vitals & rules**, review **Level**, **Armor Class**, **Maximum HP** and **Current HP**, entering your actual values.

**Why it matters:** HP and Armor Class are manual. Level updates proficiency and total hit dice, but does not fill HP or spell slots.

**Control or area:** **Maximum HP** in **Vitals & rules**

**Expected result:** The fields show your intended values after you leave them. Maximum HP above zero makes healing available.

### Find the at-table controls

On **Play**, locate **Amount**, **Damage**, **Heal** and **Checks & saves**; use them only for a real game event.

**Why it matters:** Damage uses temporary HP first. Healing is capped by maximum HP. Conditions are reminders, not automatic rule adjustments.

**Control or area:** **Amount** in **Hit points**

**Expected result:** You can find HP and check/save controls without changing the character. **Undo** is available after a reversible action.

### Read a roll without applying damage

Optionally open **Dice**, enter 1d20 and choose **Roll**; then close the dice dialog.

**Why it matters:** This adds a real roll-history result, not damage or a resource change. You may skip the practice roll.

**Control or area:** **Dice** / dice expression

**Expected result:** The result appears in the dice dialog/history, or you continue without rolling.

### Know where the rest lives

Visit **Spells**, **Gear** or **Journal** with the navigation. On phones, **More** also opens **Map** and **Edit character**.

**Why it matters:** Spells and gear have explicit item forms; the Journal has saved documents. Map tools appear only after adding a map.

**Control or area:** Character navigation; **More** on phones

**Expected result:** You can open another section and return to **Play**. No spell, item or map needs to be added for this step.

### Keep a copy outside the browser

Choose **More** → **Export this character with media**, review **Backup coverage**, then choose **Export file** and check the saved file.

**Why it matters:** Browser saving is not an external backup. Export includes available media; review missing-media warnings.

**Control or area:** **More** / **Export this character with media** / **Export file**

**Expected result:** You have checked a downloaded JSON file outside browser storage. A prepared-file message alone does not verify that.

### Help stays within reach

Open Help from the header or More whenever you need a task, or restart this walkthrough from the Help page.

**Why it matters:** Your real changes remain. Closing, skipping or restarting instructions never deletes a character or undoes your work.

**Control or area:** Help in the header

**Expected result:** The searchable task guide opens when you finish.
